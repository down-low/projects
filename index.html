Tour Schedule

SFFD 31-day watch rotation · positions from Vacancy List 26 A-071

    Each date maps to a watch day (1–31) on a continuous cycle: 1 Jan 2026 is watch 16, 1 Jan 2027 is watch 9. Each tour works 9 watches out of 31. A position's tour is the two digits before the final M in its position number (29EOC31M is Tour 31). Swing positions show the company they cover on each watch. Where the vacancy list gives a starred replacement name, only that name is shown. Editors can record tour changes (from an effective date) and leave: VA vacation, S sick, D disabled. A person on leave still appears on their scheduled days, marked off.
