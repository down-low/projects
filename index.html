      Add a person not on the vacancy list

      For relief, detailed or newly hired staff. Once added, give them a cover assignment so they appear on the schedule.

      Each date maps to a watch day (1–31) on a continuous cycle: 1 Jan 2026 is watch 16, 1 Jan 2027 is watch 9. Each tour works 9 watches out of 31. A position's tour is the two digits before the final M in its position number (29EOC31M is Tour 31). Swing positions show the company they cover on each watch. Where the vacancy list gives a starred replacement name, only that name is shown. Editors can record tour changes (from an effective date), leave (VA vacation, S sick, D disabled) and temporary cover assignments: the person covering works the covered position's tour and company, shown as "Acting for" the holder. A person on leave still appears on their scheduled days, marked off. "Spot open for bid" marks positions the vacancy list shows as vacant while the listed person still works them.
